# Orbit brand

## Who Orbit is for

Orbit is for overwhelmed adults who cannot keep a routine. The friction, not the diagnosis, defines
the audience.

In that person's words: "I know what I need to do, but I cannot keep the routine going. I forget,
miss a day, lose the thread, and find it harder to start again. When everything piles up, setting up
or maintaining a system becomes another task I cannot finish."

Orbit must help that person start, remember, act, and restart after a miss without making the system
itself another source of work.

## Who Orbit is not for

Orbit is not for a generic "everyone who wants to be more productive" audience. A diagnosis neither
qualifies nor disqualifies someone. The stated buyer is defined by the routine friction they feel.

**The founder is not the ICP, and his usage pattern is not evidence of demand.** Thomas uses Orbit
manual-first and reserves AI for bulk work. That behavior must not be used to make the manual path
the product. The one conclusion that survives from his usage is that bulk is where AI beats manual
outright.

## Positioning

**Orbit is an AI that tracks habits, not a habit tracker with an AI.**

The AI is the product, not a feature hidden in a menu. A design that reads as a list app with a chat
tab has failed this positioning. Astra must be part of how a person starts, understands, changes,
and acts on a routine, rather than an optional destination outside the main experience.

## Brand principles

1. **Make the next action easier than maintaining the system.** A primary screen violates this
   principle when setup, organisation, or explanation competes with the action the person came to
   take.
2. **Design for restarting, not only for staying perfect.** A missed routine violates this principle
   when the screen turns it into punishment, a dead end, or more work instead of making the return
   path clear.
3. **Put AI on the primary path.** A flow violates this principle when Astra can be removed without
   changing how the person starts, understands, changes, or acts on a routine.
4. **Let one instruction replace repeated work.** A flow violates this principle when a person must
   repeat the same edit across habits even though Astra could complete the bulk change from one
   request.

## Relationship to marketing

The stated buyer does not need an ADHD diagnosis. Marketing still speaks from Thomas's own ADHD
because that is the founder's true story, and store search metadata keeps its ADHD keywords. Those
keywords answer what someone searched for; they do not redefine who qualifies for Orbit.

ADHD may support the story, but the standing promise names the friction. "ADHD-friendly" may be a
supporting claim; "for people with ADHD" is not the default headline.

Orbit and Astra are never translated.

## Boundary with DESIGN.md

`BRAND.md` defines the audience, positioning, and principles that a design must satisfy. `DESIGN.md`
defines the mechanical UI guideline that implements them. Visual and interaction specifications
belong in `DESIGN.md`, not here.
