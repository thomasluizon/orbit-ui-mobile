screen  Perfil exists to change one setting and get out. The top level holds the account row, the Orbit Pro position, then Preferências, Orbit and Notificações. Each opens one sub-screen with a title and a visible back control. Mais do Orbit stays inline, and sign out stays last. There is no avatar, bio or stats.

account and preferences  The account row opens Conta: edit name, export, usage analytics, fresh start, then account deletion with a danger label, confirmation and step up. Preferências holds time zone, week start, clock, language, theme and the general habits switch. Every setting has one home.

orbit  The allowance panel shows the remainder and a bar over the count of the day: 5 on free, 50 on Pro. It never states when the allowance returns, because no endpoint returns that moment. Free sees Pro, which leaves for Orbit Pro. Pro has no upgrade action. Pro gets the billing hand off, which leaves for Orbit Assinatura. Billing is NOT a step up, because the Stripe customer portal authenticates the person.

keys  A free account sees the description and a lock row that routes to the upgrade and nothing else. The step up is reached only on Pro, and then the list. Keys and account deletion are the only two step up operations.

notifications  The product email consent is one nullable field, so never asked is a real third state. It shows as a question with two answers, not as a toggle in a default nobody chose. Push is a per device registration capped at 5, so the group states the count against the cap.

more  Calendar sync keeps its Pro badge and upgrade redirect on free accounts and opens Calendário on Pro.

ending  Sign out is the last top level row and leaves for Entrar. Account deletion is last in Conta, and it routes to step up. The step up for API keys returns to Orbit. Cancelled deletion returns to Conta.

plan  Free is the default, because most accounts are free. Three gates draw from both sides: the allowance panel, the keys section and the calendar sync row. Every gated tap leaves for Orbit Pro through one route. A gated control is never a disabled row and never a silent no-op.

toggles  A switch is labelled for its on state, never as a negation.

accent  Exactly one filled action at a time: the consent answer while the question is unanswered, the confirmation once one is open. The key actions stay neutral. On wide, the sidebar create is the one filled action.
