screen  Perfil exists to change one setting and get out. Five headings: you, Astra, notifications, more of Orbit, and an ending heading at the bottom for the things that end something. It is still not a profile page: no avatar, no bio, no stats, and no home for anything that belongs on another screen. The fourth group is routes only, because PROFILE_NAV_ITEMS (profile-navigation.ts) is the app's own list of where Perfil goes: preferences and ai-settings folded into this screen, achievements went to Progresso (D69 item 8), the retrospective into Astra's line (D69 item 12), and the remaining four, Wrapped, about, the Android widget and calendar sync, were dead ends in this project until this group.

states  default, email never asked, email on, email off, allowance spent, exporting, step up, step up done, keys none, fresh start, delete, loading. A plan axis sits beside the state, free or Pro, defaulting to free: a plan is an account fact that survives a state change, so free and pro came OFF the state axis in the same edit that added it. Reached by using the screen rather than the axis: the rename sheet from the account row, the revoke confirmation from a key's own action, the scoped create from its button, and the export's preparing beat from the row itself.

you  the account row carries the display name, opens the rename (edit-name-sheet.tsx, opened from profile-identity-header.tsx:53) and states the address under it: the name is editable in the app and nothing decided otherwise, so the earlier "no display name" line in this report was wrong and is gone. Then the settings that drive every dated screen: timezone and the 24 hour clock (profile.ts:23 and :24, both real writable fields), week start, language. Theme is a real setting (themePreference, profile.ts:50, written through /api/profile/theme-preference, endpoints.ts:24) with exactly two values, themeModeSchema = z.enum(['dark','light']) (profile.ts:131), so it is drawn as a two way choice with no system option, and choosing one turns the screen itself. colorScheme (profile.ts:51) stays deleted: two modes, no hue. Show general habits on Hoje is the real switch at preference-settings-list.tsx:91 with its description. Export my data (use-data-export.ts, profile-account-actions.tsx:36) sits last in the group with its preparing state drawn, and a done toast in the shell's notice slot; the composer chip stopped being the only way to reach it.

astra  the daily allowance with its remainder, a bar over the day's count, 5 on free against 50 on Pro (D70 item 1). It never states when the allowance returns, because no endpoint returns that moment. Free carries see Pro, which leaves for Orbit Pro; Pro carries no upgrade action, since there is nothing above it, and gets the billing hand off instead, which leaves for Orbit Assinatura: billing is NOT a step up, because the Stripe customer portal authenticates the person itself. Then the two things Astra does unasked, each labelled for its on state.

keys  the section matches advanced/page.tsx:81-110. The heading carries the Pro badge on both sides. A free account sees the section's description and a lock row that ROUTES to the upgrade and nothing else, so the step up is only ever reached on Pro. A Pro account reaches the step up first (keys and account deletion are the only two step up operations; StepUp structurally cannot hold a credential field), and after it the list: each key a row with its name, its mono prefix and a REVOKE through ListRow's real per-row action with its own confirmation, then create and scoped create, and the MCP connection line under them. The exact scope vocabulary and the MCP snippet are the app's own, so the scoped create takes the scope as text and the MCP line names where the key goes without inventing an endpoint.

notifications  one per user field, the product email consent, nullable (profile.ts:71), so never asked is a real third state and renders as a question with two answers rather than a toggle resting in a default nobody chose. Everything else is a per device push registration capped at 5, so the group states the count against the cap and names the device in hand. Each habit's reminders live on the habit, and the group says so instead of drawing a global toggle with nothing to write to.

more  plain rows, a route and not a home: Orbit Wrapped opens the Wrapped document, about opens Sobre, the Android widget opens its document with the widget hint as the row's description (advanced/page.tsx:71-74), support opens the conversation, because support runs through Astra's send_support_request tool, and calendar sync (D70 item 4) follows the plan: the Pro badge and the upgrade route on free, the sync boundary that Orbit Calendario already draws on Pro. Three documents in this project stopped being unreachable when this group landed.

ending  sign out leaves for Entrar. Fresh start (fresh-start-modal.tsx, profile-account-actions.tsx:54) ends something without ending the account, so it sits under this heading above delete, with its own confirmation naming what it clears and what stays. Delete account keeps the status-bad label, the last position, and one confirmation that says there is no way back rather than implying one.

plan  free is the default because it is the account most people have and the harder case to get right. Three gates draw from both sides: the allowance panel, the keys section and the calendar sync row. Every gated tap leaves for Orbit Pro through one route; a gated affordance is never a disabled row and never a silent no-op. The state axis jumped to a key state on a free account shows the lock row, because that is all a free account can reach.

toggles  a switch is labelled for its on state, so the label reads as what happens when it is on and never as a negation. Nothing here has to be mentally inverted.

spacing  4 8 12 16 24 32, gap only. Group gap 32 against in-group 12, which is what makes five headings read as five groups. Rows keep 16 of inline padding and a 44 minimum.
