screen  Calendário answers where the time actually went. Month, week, range and agenda are four views of one content, switched with SegmentedControl. It is an orientation view and a way to fix one day, not a second habit list: one ring per day, never one per habit per day.

views  Agenda lists the days ahead and is drawn at both widths, because a whole view is not navigation chrome and parity binds it. Range is a fourteen day span paged as a span. Paging works by swipe as well as by the chevrons.

ring  the month response has no per day status, so every ring is arithmetic done on the device. No cell carries a word like good or bad. Four shapes carry the outcomes and colour only reinforces them: no ring for nothing scheduled, a hollow ring for nothing logged, a partial arc for part done, a filled neutral disc for fully logged. The arc is never rounded. Completion is neutral, so the full day is fg-1.

boundary  only the last seven days can be logged. A loggable day is a DayCell button that requires onPress, and every other day is role img and rejects one. The seven loggable cells sit on a raised well, and the legend names the difference. The read only day card has no completion control and no explanation, because nothing failed. Paging is not bounded.

excluded  a habit with no schedule never appears, and a sub habit's completion never reaches a cell.

freeze  no freeze is drawn here. The history the API serves reaches back thirty days while this grid pages back further. Freezes live on Progresso.

pro  Calendar sync keeps its Pro gate. Connection, last sync and automatic sync live only in the Google Calendar disclosure and never occupy the day card. At most three imported events appear in the day card, followed by an entry to all events.

accent  current position is the Calendário tab, today's ring and the selected cell. Unfinished progress is the partial arc. The ordinary state has no filled action.
