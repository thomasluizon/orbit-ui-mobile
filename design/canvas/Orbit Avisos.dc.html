screen  Avisos answers what did I miss. It is a list of things that happened while the person was away, and every row leads somewhere. The payload carries a title, a body, a link, an optional habit, a read flag and a timestamp, and the row renders all six and nothing invented.

states  unread, all read, one item, full list, detail, delete queued, clear all, empty, loading. Hover, focus-visible and press are live. No plan axis: this screen has no gate.

bell  one control, two placements: the mobile shell header and the web sidebar. The count is a neutral pill, fg-1 with canvas text, because an unread count is not a next action, a position or progress. At zero the pill is absent. The count is capped in copy at 9 and over.

read  read and unread differ by more than colour: an unread row carries a filled dot in a reserved column, its title is a weight step up, and it sits on the card surface while a read row sits flat on the canvas. The dot column is reserved on every row, so nothing shifts when a row is read. Mark all read is in the options menu and absent at zero unread. It queues while offline.

acts  a row opens the notification's own sheet: the mono timestamp eyebrow with the target, the body in full, and three actions. View is the primary and leads to the stated target. Mark as read is absent once the row is read. Delete is destructive and last. Each row also carries a real 44px delete, a sibling of the row body, its glyph in status-bad paired with the word in its accessible name. The delete is queued: the row leaves at once, a neutral Toast offers the undo, and the toast leaves when the queue commits or the undo lands, never on its own timer. Clear all is absent when the list is empty. It asks first, names how many go, and says there is no way back.

links  every link points at one of the four destinations or at a habit, and the row states its target in mono. The streak notification goes to Progresso.

empty  the mark at 96, one line, and no action, because nothing is left for a person to do in an empty inbox. It parks no figure and no upgrade call.

loading  the list's own shape, three lines per row, aria-busy, no spinner. The bell keeps its count while the list loads.

shell  Avisos is a pushed screen with back, title and a trailing dots-vertical options button. Mark all read and clear all live in its menu, and clear all keeps its confirmation.

accent  current position on the Hoje tab and the focus ring on the 412 shell. The sheet's view action is the one filled action while the sheet is open. The list has none, because every row is the action. Wide: the sidebar create is the filled action, so the sheet's view steps down to the neutral fill.

contrast  the unread title is fg-1 on the card and the read title is fg-2 on the canvas. The body, the timestamp and the target are fg-2, because the unread row is a hover child inside a card wrapper and fg-3 and fg-4 fall under their floors on its hovered surface. The target icon is fg-3.

open  whether a notification whose habit was deleted should render at all, since the row would lead nowhere.
