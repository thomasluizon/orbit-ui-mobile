screen  The pitch is one sentence: ten times more Orbit, the true multiple of 50 over 5. That sentence is the screen, at display size, above the card that proves it. It is not a feature matrix. There is no plan axis, because this screen has no gate: it is where the gates lead. A Pro account that opens this route gets Orbit Assinatura. There is no start trial state, because nothing can start a trial.

arithmetic  The comparison is two numbers side by side, 5 a day against 50 a day, on one card. The note underneath says what the free number buys, not what it costs.

outcomes  Each tier card has four outcomes: 50 Orbit messages a day instead of 5, Google Calendar beside habits, a weekly and monthly review, and Orbit noticing without being asked. Goals stay free and are not sold here.

prices  No price is typed in this drawing. The server returns two amounts, a currency, a savings percent and an optional coupon percent, and every figure comes from that response. A missing response is a state: the tiers carry their own loading shape and their own error with a retry.

interval  A segmented control chooses monthly or annual, so choosing an interval is not choosing a plan. Annual is the default and carries the one recommended badge.

tiers  There are two tiers. The CTA verb is identical on every tier, so the tiers differ by price and not by how hard they ask.

decline  Decline is as reachable as accept: a plain inline link at the end, 16px and underlined. Free is that link and not a third card, because a card would make staying free look like a purchase.

trial  The paywall never says something the next line contradicts. Inside a trial, the heading is the arithmetic from the other direction: the 50 a day stay, or go back to 5. The eyebrow has three variants: the free line, the day count, and LAST DAY as its own string. Nothing offers to start anything.

reassurance  Five lines say what the shipping pitch says before anyone pays: the promise, the trust line, cancel anytime, the renewal note and the annual hero line. The trust line shows ONLY outside a trial.

hand off  Buying is a hand off and not a step up: the Stripe portal authenticates the person. No card field, address field or credential appears here. There is no rewarded ad and no bonus message control.

accent  One filled action per view: the CTA of the selected tier. The other CTA is the same verb in ghost. The accent never lands on the arithmetic, the outcomes or the badge.
