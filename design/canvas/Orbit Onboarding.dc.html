screen  Onboarding produces one real habit that the person typed, and ends with the person looking at it. It is not a tour, a quiz or a survey. The feature guide lives on the About screen.

position  One indicator: the mono counter Orbit · 01 / 03, over three steps. An sr-only progress element gives a screen reader the same position. Back shows on the two steps that have a step behind them. Skip shows on every step except the last and goes straight to the end, so the person is never trapped on the first screen.

decisions  Three decisions, and no more: what you want to keep doing, when it happens, and whether Orbit may remind you. The person types the first, taps to correct the proposed second, and answers the third with yes or no. The person can change everything else later from the habit.

input  Use one Input: multiline, three rows, with marks and a marksLabel for the words the parser consumed. The field has maxLength 100 and no counter. The quick start chips fill the field, so the habit stays the sentence of the person.

failure  When creating the habit fails, the form keeps the typed sentence and the chosen days. A neutral Toast in the notice slot states the failure and the fix, and the pinned action becomes the retry.

permission  Ask only after the habit exists. The preview shows the real notification: the habit title and the relative time, such as Due in 15 minutes. A habit with no time gets no ask. The step says that reminders need a time and offers to go back. The ask has four outcomes: granted, refused, already refused and enable failed. Refused costs nothing. Already refused points to the system settings. Enable failed makes retry the pinned action, because the habit is already saved. The allow control is absent in the failure shapes and in the no time shape.

order  The three decisions pass nav false, so the shell draws no tab bar and no sidebar. The last step returns to the plain nav shape. Each step puts its one forward action in the pinned action slot.

delight  One ring sweep as the habit lands: 280 ms, stroke offset only, no fill mode. Nothing else in the flow animates its own entrance.

limit  Reading the sentence spends one Orbit message. At the daily limit, the notice states the allowance and the schedule is not proposed. The day pills and the time field carry the second decision with no model call.

accent  One filled action per step: the forward action. Back and skip are ghost controls. A selected day pill takes the selected tint and ring, not a fill. The final ring lands neutral, because the habit is not complete on the day it is created.
