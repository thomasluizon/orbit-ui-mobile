screen  Progresso answers am I moving. It composes what used to be three screens, so the thing that keeps it one answer is a single descending column: the streak is the focal element, at 60px, bare on the canvas with no card around it, because it is the only thing here in the present tense and the only thing carrying an action. Goals come second as the toward what. Four figures come third in one window. Achievements come last, smallest type on the screen. Nothing is a card unless it is a separable object, so the four sections carry only a section title and space between them, never a panel each.

not  not a trophy cabinet and not a chart gallery. No chart, no new shape: every figure is StatTile, ProgressBar or ProgressRing, and nothing needed a shape the system cannot draw.

states  default, gap, no freeze, bank full, frozen today, goal at 100, abandoned goal, goal detail, linked at limit, loading, error, empty, Astra at limit. A plan axis sits beside the state, free or Pro, defaulting to free: a plan is an account fact that survives a state change, so it never sits on the state axis. Reached by using the screen rather than the axis: the goal detail by tapping a goal, the filters and their empty case by tapping completed, the edit sheet, the reopen on an abandoned goal, the history’s show all, and reordering by dragging a goal card.

record  a record is not a next action, so the accent is absent from every finished thing: an earned achievement is neutral, the streak total is neutral, and a goal at 100 renders status-done as a filled disc with a check rather than a ring at full sweep, so there is no intermediate flip between the two shapes. Below 100 the ring sweeps primary over the status-empty track. The accent enters only on progress toward something unfinished.

streak  one streak per person, never one per habit. Three figures, the shipping three (StreakStatsRow, streak-sections.tsx:46-61): the current streak as the focal 60px number, and the longest streak and the tier as two tiles under the strip; the tier’s vocabulary lives in streakDisplay.detail.tierTileLabel, so the word drawn is a fixture of that ladder. The strip carries exactly four derived values, active frozen missed today, and under it the legend names its marks in words (StreakTimelineCard): day active, day frozen, day missed, because colour and shape alone are not carriers. The freeze bank is drawn as FreezeProgressCard draws it: freezes banked over the ceiling of 3, freezes used this month, and the days toward the next freeze as a bar over the 7 day earn rate, resting while the bank is full. Protected days list each frozen day by date, protected today included, with their own empty line when none exist. When today itself is frozen the frozen banner says so above the strip (streak-frozen-banner.tsx, mounted at page.tsx:88); the earlier claim here that today can never be frozen contradicted that mounted banner and is gone. An unlogged today still renders as today, not as missed, and the number stands while the day is open.

repair  the person comes back, the surface shows the gap, and spending a freeze on it is an action they take. Nothing is applied silently on their behalf. The copy names the circumstance and never the person, states how many days are open and how many freezes exist, and the button states how many it will spend. When the bank is empty there is no action and no blame: the line says what happens next and when the next freeze arrives. The bank ceiling is 3, one earned every 7 streak days, and a full bank is the one genuine at capacity state here: neutral, stating the limit, no upgrade action.

plan  free is the default because it is the account most people have and the harder case to get right. Two gates draw from both sides, and both state which way the decisions point: achievements are hard gated on Pro in the shipping code, so a free account sees the locked state (achievements-locked-state.tsx) with the route to Orbit Pro and the line naming api #332 as the ungating; the four figures come from the retrospective, whose endpoint defaults Pro (PayGateService.cs:141, RetrospectiveProOnly), so the window section gets the same two sided treatment. Goals are ungated and stay ungated: D70 item 3 moved them out of the paywall, and a gate a decision removed does not come back. Every gated tap leaves for Orbit Pro through one route; a gated affordance is never a disabled control and never a silent no-op.

goals  three statuses and no others, active completed abandoned, under four views of one list: all, active, completed, abandoned (goals-view.tsx:29-32), a SegmentedControl because they are views of the same thing. A filter that matches nothing gets its own empty line and an action that clears the filter (:55-58); completed shows it against this fixture, since no goal here has been completed yet. Goals reorder by dragging with the whole card as the handle, the same gesture Hoje’s list uses: hold and move, at 300ms with a 5px tolerance on touch and a 5px drag with a pointer, the order written on release (ReorderGoalsCommand, the reorder_goals tool); dragging runs on the unfiltered list. An active goal carries one tracking word, on track at risk behind or no deadline, and an abandoned goal reports nothing: no ring, no tracking word, no figure. No goal has a colour, an icon or an emoji. There is no create entry: a goal is made from a habit or by asking, which is why the empty state’s action routes to a habit rather than to a form. The detail gained the rest of the shipping surface: the goal’s own progress history with its show all toggle (goal-detail-sections.tsx:30-111), the linked habits section (:216), and the full footer, edit, mark abandoned, delete with a confirmation naming what goes, and REOPEN on an abandoned goal, which exists twice in the code (goal-action-footer.tsx:55 renders reactivate, UpdateGoalStatusCommand.cs:44 runs goal.Reactivate(), and RestoreGoalCommand.cs is its own command), so the earlier open question claiming no restore path was false and is deleted.

derived  only a streak goal derives its progress, from the smallest current streak among its linked habits, so it hides the manual input and the surface names what the number comes from. Every other goal keeps its manual input however many habits are linked, because that input is the only control that can move it. A derived value is not a proposed value: it renders like a typed one and wears no dashed hairline. That is today's behaviour and the drawing is right. D69's wording says a goal with linked habits derives its progress, so the rule is half implemented on the api side; ticket #340 on the api carries the gap, and until it lands the manual input on a linked goal is correct rather than a bug.

completion  a streak goal can sit at 100 while still active, because the read path refreshes the value and never changes the status. So completion is drawn as a write the person makes: at 100 an active goal shows one neutral action that finishes it, with a line stating why it is not automatic. The action is the fg-1 fill rather than the accent, because the accent never marks completion, which also leaves the repair as the only accent-filled action on the screen.

achievements  grouped, not flat: achievements/page.tsx:96-99 maps achievementsByCategory through a section per category, so the grid carries a heading at each category start; the category names here are fixtures of that grouping. The XP row stays exactly as ruled on 2026-08-20: one row above the grid, level and title on one line, the figure on the other end, one bar beneath. Earned and unearned differ by shape before colour: earned is a filled status-done disc with the glyph knocked out, unearned a hollow ring at hairline-strong with the glyph at fg-3. In progress is derived from a current over a target, both null on a one shot achievement, so that tile has no bar rather than an empty one. Social, Sharing and Together stay hidden, and stopping the API from returning them is the real fix, api #332. Nothing is secret: every name and description shows while unearned.

xp  a row, not a ring: the level and its title on one line, the figure on the other end, one bar under them. The ladder has no cap, so past level 10 the number keeps climbing and the title stays the one level 10 carries.

motion  no entrance animation and no stagger, because this is reached many times a day and the frequency gate rules it out. The only motion is hover, press, and the ring and bar sweeps, which indicate state rather than announce arrival.

spacing  4 8 12 16 24 32 48, gap only. Section gap 32 against in-section 12, which is the 2x rule. 44 is the touch and stepper token, 56 the list row, 32 the achievement indicator, 20 and 24 the strip cell.

radius  8 on badges and strip cells, 12 on the gap well and the notice, 20 on cards and tiles, 28 on the shell and the sheet, 999 on interactive and on the two round indicators.

accent  412 default: no filled action at all, which is correct for a surface that reports. Current position is the Progresso tab and the strip's today cell. Progress is the unfinished goal rings and the XP bar. In the gap state the freeze spend is the one filled action; in the empty state the one action is; the two never coexist. The goal completion action and the wide width's filled actions step down to the neutral fg-1 fill, since the sidebar create owns the fill there. Completion is neutral everywhere: the 100 percent disc, the earned achievement and the full bar are all status-done.

contrast  white on primary 4.57:1 rest, 5.11:1 hover #B74E12, 6.09:1 pressed #A24716. primary-soft on the canvas 4.58:1, used only for the active nav item and nowhere above the canvas. Light status-overdue #946A00 is 4.66:1 on #FAFAFA and white on it 4.86:1, unused here since no habit status renders on this screen. The three known limits are left as they are; limit 3 is touched by the unearned achievement ring, so that ring is drawn in hairline-strong rather than fg-4.

decided  1 the retrospective survives (D69, item 12): Astra delivers it on a cadence, into the proactive line, with no navigation entry of its own. What D69 deleted is its dedicated screen and its three dead states, not the thing itself, so this build loses no surface. 2 the 30 day window is stated by code and the match is exact: the four figures are RetrospectiveMetrics' CompletionRate, ActiveDays, WeeklyConsistency and TopHabits (GetRetrospectiveQuery.cs:21-31), and RetrospectivePeriodRange.Resolve computes the month window as today.AddDays(-30) (RetrospectivePeriodRange.cs:28) ending today (:35), thirty rolling days, which is what this screen draws. HabitMetricsCalculator.cs:28,29 corroborates the convention from the other side, a weekly rate over 7 days and a monthly one over 30.
