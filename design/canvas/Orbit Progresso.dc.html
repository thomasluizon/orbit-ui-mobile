screen  Progresso answers: am I moving. It is one descending column. The streak is the focal element, at 60px, bare on the canvas with no card, because it is the only thing in the present tense and the only thing with an action. Goals come second, four figures third, achievements last in the smallest type. Nothing is a card unless it is a separable object, so the sections carry a title and space, never a panel each.

record  A record is not a next action, so no accent marks a finished thing. An earned achievement and the streak total are neutral. A goal at 100 renders status-done as a filled disc with a check, not as a ring at full sweep. The accent enters only on progress toward something unfinished.

streak  The current streak and the fourteen day strip form the stable summary. One labelled Freezes row opens a sheet at both widths. While history is pending, the row hides its value, keeps its space and disables the entry.

repair  The person comes back, the surface shows the gap, and spending a freeze on it is an action the person takes. Nothing is applied silently. The copy names the circumstance, never the person. When the bank is empty there is no action and no blame: the line says when the next freeze arrives. A full bank is neutral and has no upgrade action.

plan  Free is the default, because it is the harder case to get right. Achievements and the four figures are gated. A free account sees a locked card with the route to Orbit Pro. Goals are ungated. A gated control is never disabled and never a silent no-op.

goals  A goal has three statuses: active, completed and abandoned. One menu holds four filters: all, active, completed and abandoned. A filter with no match shows its own empty line and an action that clears the filter. Goals reorder by dragging with the whole card as the handle. An abandoned goal shows no ring, no tracking word and no figure. No goal has a colour, an icon or an emoji. There is no create entry: a goal is made from a habit or by asking, so the empty state action routes to a habit. Reopen is available on an abandoned goal.

derived  Only a streak goal derives its progress, from the smallest current streak among its linked habits. It hides the manual input and names the source of the number. Every other goal keeps its manual input, because that input is the only control that can move it. A derived value renders like a typed value.

completion  A streak goal can sit at 100 while active, because reading never changes the status. So completion is a write the person makes: at 100 an active goal shows one neutral action that finishes it, with a line that says why it is not automatic. The action uses the fg-1 fill, because the accent never marks completion.

achievements  The grid is grouped, with a heading at each category start. Earned and unearned differ by shape before colour: earned is a filled status-done disc with the glyph knocked out, and unearned is a hollow ring at hairline-strong. A one shot achievement has no progress bar. Social, Sharing and Together stay hidden. Every name and description shows while unearned.

xp  The XP row is a row, not a ring: level and title on one line, the figure on the other end, one bar under them. The ladder has no cap, so past level 10 the title stays the one of level 10.

motion  There is no entrance animation, because the person reaches this screen many times a day. The only motion is hover, press, and the ring and bar sweeps.

accent  At 412 the default has no filled action, which is right for a surface that reports. In the gap state the freeze spend is the one filled action. At wide width every filled action steps down to the neutral fg-1 fill, because the sidebar create owns the fill.
