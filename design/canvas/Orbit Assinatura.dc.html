screen  Assinatura answers what am I paying for, and lets a person change or stop it. It is the primary billing screen for people who bought.

states  Stripe, Play, trial, lifetime, canceled, past due, lapsed, portal opening, portal failed, offline, load failed, loading. A free account sees Orbit Pro, not this screen.

two paths  the Stripe and Play variants differ in structure. A Google Play subscription cannot open the Stripe portal, so the Play variant sends the person to the Play dashboard. The Play variant offers no portal. The Stripe variant never mentions Play.

portal  opening the Stripe portal can fail on its own. While it opens, the action shows its loading state. A failure states what happened, states the fix and offers a retry. This state differs from a subscription that does not load, and the drawing shows both.

billing  the billing dashboard is drawn in the app. The invoice history is one row per invoice with a reason, a status (paid, open, void) and a download on the ListRow action. The payment method states the card brand, last digits and expiry. The usage figure shows AI messages against the ceiling.

lifetime  read only, and it says so: no portal, no cancel, no interval and no action, because nothing can change. It states what it is and stops.

lapsed  a lapsed plan must not read as free with no account of what changed. It says the subscription ended, names the day, and lists what is no longer on, in a neutral tone with no alarm colour. One action subscribes again and leaves for Orbit Pro. The reason it ended shows only when the person can act on it, as a circumstance and never as blame. The reason line carries data-mock, since no endpoint produces it yet.

canceled  canceled and past due are badged states and neither is lapsed. A canceled plan still runs and will not renew, so its line says when access ends. A past due plan keeps running while the provider retries. Both keep the portal action and neither has an alarm colour.

trial  a trial reads as plan pro with a separate trial flag, so the badge derives from both. The badge states the trial and the facts state the day count. Nothing offers to start or extend a trial.

hand off  billing is a hand off and not a step up, because the Stripe portal authenticates the person itself. No card, address or credential field appears, so the action is a single button and not a form.

offline  mobile renders offline here, so the action is disabled with its reason beside it. The plan facts stay visible.

accent  at most one filled action per state, and several states have none. Lifetime has no action. The badge is the neutral well, not an accent chip.

decided  the Play variant deep links to the specific subscription, on the condition that the implementing ticket confirms the URL shape against the Google documentation.
