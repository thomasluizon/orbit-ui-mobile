screen  Four static screens: about, privacy, terms and support. Each has the purpose and the entry order of the screen it replaces. There is no plan axis: nothing here is gated, and the terms page that names Pro describes billing and is not a gate.

labels  The about navigation labels and their order are load bearing: feature guide, support, terms, privacy. The feature guide is the drawer of About, opened from its first row. Do not add a row beside the four, because an invented row is a promise that the product must keep.

prose  Privacy and terms share one layout: a title, the date of the last change, then sections of a heading and a short paragraph, capped near 62 characters. The legal copy is a layout placeholder. The final wording is the legal one.

support  Support is a form, so it ships every control state: the subject picker and the message field in default, focused, filled, invalid and disabled, the account field disabled with its reason beside it, and the submit in default, disabled and loading. Errors sit next to their field and state the fix. THE DRAFT SURVIVES LEAVING: the app keeps the subject and the message on the device on every keystroke and clears them only after a successful send. Offline refuses the send: the submit is disabled with a reason beside it, and the reason says the words stay on the device. The subject is free text, so the options are authored phrasings and there are no categories.

success  Success is a designed state, not a toast: a check, one heading, one line that says where the answer arrives, and one quiet action back. There is NO reference number, because nothing returns one. It states a working day, not a time.

failure  A send that fails keeps every word the person typed, says that nothing was sent, and offers the retry. The text is never cleared on error.

accent  At most one filled action per state: the submit on the form, the retry on a failure, none on the three read only screens. Wide keeps the sidebar create as the one filled action, so the submit steps down to the neutral fill.
