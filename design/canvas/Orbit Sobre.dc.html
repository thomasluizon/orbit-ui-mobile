clipping  the inherited defect was the about screen clipping horizontally at 412 in Portuguese, in both themes, and it is fixed by structure rather than by shortening the strings. Every label and value pair wraps rather than competing for one line: the row is a wrapping flex, the label takes the remaining space with a zero min width, the value may fall to its own line, and long unbroken values break inside a word rather than pushing the row wider. The name and the version are stacked rather than inline, and every column in the tree declares a zero min width, so no descendant can force the scroller wider than the shell.

An earlier pass dropped the feature guide row claiming D69 deleted it; D69 does not mention the feature guide.
