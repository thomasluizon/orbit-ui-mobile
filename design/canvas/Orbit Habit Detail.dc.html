screen  One surface, two documents: this one is the authority for the habit detail, and Hoje's detail state is the same surface reached from Hoje. The order is the shipping drawer's (habit-detail-drawer.tsx): the header, the interactive checklist, the three numbers, the reminders it holds, the end date, the linked goals, the month calendar and the ask Astra row, with the redesign's strip and slipping line up top, then the editable half behind ONE disclosure, the immutable start date, and delete last.

states  holding, slipping, sub habits, checklist, no data, metrics loading, delete, loading, error, at the daily limit. A plan axis sits beside the state, free or Pro, defaulting to free, and it moves exactly three gates: the reschedule, add a sub habit, and the slip alert inside the disclosure. Renaming, the emoji picker, the disclosure, a sub habit as the subject and the delete question are reached by using the screen rather than the axis.

header  a 76px emoji well, the name with rename in place, a summary line, and every tag as a plain word pill. The well IS the emoji control (habit-form-fields.tsx:242 renders HabitEmojiSelector): tapping it opens a picker with a search that clears, the eleven category chips from habit-emoji-options.ts, and a grid; with no emoji the well shows a plus, because here it is an empty control and not a label. The tags lose the shipping colour dot deliberately: colour as data is dead (DESIGN.md:737) and a habit is told apart by its emoji, its name and its ring. The header ring is a real 44px button on HabitRow's pairing, its accessible name the action and its direction, the ring inside it decorative. Logging from here resets the checklist ONLY under Habit.cs:186-190: a frequency, not flexible, the due date advances; a flexible habit's list does not reset.

strip  DayStrip in its habit scope, 30 days, the same period as the third number, so the picture and the number describe one window. A freeze marks a user and a date (StreakFreeze.Create), never a habit, so frozen is absent from the habit union and no snowflake can appear here; the freeze lives on Progresso where the day is the subject.

numbers  the shipping three (HabitDetailStatsGrid): the current streak, labelled days free on a habit to avoid, the longest streak, and the 30 day rate, which IS monthlyCompletionRate (HabitMetricsCalculator.cs:29). Three states: the numbers, a loading row of three tiles, and ONE centred no data line when metrics is null, never three zeros. The grid renders only when the habit has a frequency or is a general one, so a one time task draws none.

slipping  drawn from the numbers, not a status: the streak at zero, the rate down, nothing logged for three days. The line names the circumstance and never the person, and it carries no red. The reschedule appears only here, because the app offers it only on an overdue habit (habit-list.tsx:906), and it needs Pro (reschedule-sheet.tsx:119): on Pro it is a proposal carrying its reason, with accept and not now; on free the same place carries the upgrade route in the shipping sheet's words, never an empty space.

checklist  ONE list with two jobs, never drawn twice at once. In the open part it is interactive: a progress bar with the mono count, uncheck everything appearing only once something is checked, clear asking first, and the last item checked on an unlogged habit asking whether to log the habit now. While the disclosure is open the same list is creation's editable one, where an item is retyped in place, duplicated, removed or dragged, with saved templates in a listed sheet. The reset rule line renders only where Habit.cs makes it true.

A child with its own children drills rather than nesting a third level inline. Add a sub habit follows the plan (startAddSubHabit, habit-list.tsx:702): on free the row carries the Pro badge and leaves for the upgrade; on Pro it would open creation, which lives in Hoje's create surface.

calendar  the whole history as a month of DayCells under MonthGrid: month back and forward, days outside the month shown as such, future days future, and a logged day tappable, answering when it was logged. The strip answers whether this one is holding right now; the calendar answers what it has looked like.

disclosure  the editable half is creation's field set (edit-habit-modal.tsx:262 renders the same HabitFormFields creation renders), behind one disclosure in creation's order: exact time, reminders, checklist items, the bad habit toggle, the slip alert, tags, goal linking, end date, description.
