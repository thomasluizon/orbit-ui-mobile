case  this one is worth interrupting someone for because each of its four triggers is a threshold the person cannot see themselves cross, and each crosses at most a few times a month, so the interruption costs less than the silence would.

screen  one celebration survives of seven. It has exactly four triggers: a streak milestone, a goal completing, a level up, and everything due today being done. An individual habit completion has no celebration. The first confirmed completion that extends today's account streak by one gets a daily streak moment instead: the previous number moves to the new number beside a day ring, in the same notice slot, with 16px padding, a 12px gap and a 28px tabular figure. It dismisses after 2500ms and its once-per-day receipt survives reloads. Past-day logs, slips, undo and unchanged streaks never trigger it. A milestone keeps the existing panel and suppresses the daily moment.

triggers  four remain the milestone and completion panel contract: CelebrationKind (celebration-queue.ts:1-6) also carries daily-streak for the transient daily moment, alongside streak, all-done, goal-completed and level-up. The streak milestone comes from AppConstants.StreakMilestoneTiers, and that list is [7, 14, 30, 90, 100, 365] (AppConstants.cs:90): the 30 drawn here is a tier, and no number outside the list may ever be drawn as one.

states  streak, goal, level, day done, queued next, reduced motion, dismissed. Each state is the same panel with a different fact in it, because a person learning one shape learns all four. Queued next is also reached by using the screen: closing the first panel promotes the second. NO plan axis: none of the four triggers is gated and none differs between plans, so the absent switch is a decision.

shape  it is a panel in the shell’s notice slot, pinned directly above the composer rather than in its place, not a modal: no scrim, nothing behind it disabled, and the day’s list stays live and tappable underneath. Skipping it is a tap on the close control or simply carrying on; it never waits for an answer. When two triggers land on the same log the panels QUEUE, one at a time: the shared store gives every kind a priority (celebration-queue.ts:61-70: streak 0, goal completed and all done 2, level up 3), sortCelebrationQueue:75 orders by that priority and then by arrival, and enqueueCelebrationItem:167 queues an item behind whatever is active, promoting the next when the active one completes. Two panels are never on screen together, and the queued next state draws exactly that: a streak panel first, the level up behind it, in the store’s own order. The host around the panel is scenery: its rows, menus, tab bar, sidebar create and palette are wired to nothing, said here once so nobody hunts for dead controls, while the panel’s own close is a real state change and the composer is Hoje’s, prop for prop, because two documents drawing one surface draw the same surface and Hoje is the authority.

motion  500ms total movement: the fully opaque panel rises 12px over 280ms ease-out while the daily figure cross-fades by 12px and the measured neutral ring sweeps once. Manual exit retargets the live position toward +12px and opacity zero over 220ms ease-in. The daily exit starts at 2280ms and retains layout through 2500ms. Hover or keyboard focus cancels automatic exit, restores the static fact and starts a fresh 2500ms schedule only after all pause reasons end. Manual exit is committed; repeated close never restarts it. It does not fade in, because a panel whose resting state is invisible disappears entirely the moment a timeline is paused or a frame is dropped, and the static cue is the whole point of this one. Nothing loops, nothing bounces, nothing throws particles.

static cue  the motion is never the message. The completed ring, the figure and the one line all state the fact and hold after the motion ends, so a person who looks up late, or whose screen was off, reads the same thing.

reduced motion  a dignified static state on both platforms, not a degraded one: the same panel, the same ring at its final value, the same line, arriving at full opacity with no rise, numeral change or sweep, and dismissing immediately on manual close. Live preference changes settle a held fact without resetting its deadline or immediately complete a pending exit. Backgrounding settles the final fact and resume compares the original deadline without replay or reannouncement. Account replacement and invalidation discard presentation and callbacks immediately.

voice  no exclamation mark anywhere, in either locale. The line states what happened and stops; it does not congratulate, does not say keep going, and does not name the person.

accent  none. Every one of the four triggers is a completion, and completion never takes the accent, so the ring lands neutral at fg-1 and the only control is a ghost close. The single sweep stays neutral throughout. A still monochrome native 16px OrbitMark sits centred in the 44px ring, never the padded artwork in a 24px box.

spacing  4 8 12 16 24, gap only. The panel is 24 of padding at 20 radius, the ring is 44, and the close control keeps its distinct 48px target and clipped hover/press fill. Daily content retains 16px padding, 12px gap and the 28px figure with a localized day unit. Text grows without clipping at 200%.

mock  every number carries data-mock: the streak day count, the goal figure, the level number, the day’s habit count and the allowance number inside the composer’s limit reason.
