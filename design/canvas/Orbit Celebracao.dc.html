case  this one is worth interrupting someone for because each of its four triggers is a threshold the person cannot see themselves cross, and each crosses at most a few times a month, so the interruption costs less than the silence would.

screen  one celebration survives of seven. It has exactly four triggers: a streak milestone, a goal completing, a level up, and everything due today being done. It never fires for an individual habit completion, which happens over a hundred times a day across a list and would put the motion frequency gate in breach on its own.

triggers  four is a DECISION, not the type’s whole list: CelebrationKind (celebration-queue.ts:1-6) has FIVE kinds, and the fifth, achievement, is absent here on purpose, because D69 item 11 names exactly four triggers. The streak milestone comes from AppConstants.StreakMilestoneTiers, and that list is [7, 14, 30, 90, 100, 365] (AppConstants.cs:90): the 30 drawn here is a tier, and no number outside the list may ever be drawn as one.

debt  the code still carries what the decision removed, and that is work the implementation owes rather than a surface this document is missing: achievement is still in CelebrationKind, achievement-toast.tsx:49 still enqueues it, and StreakFreezeCelebration still mounts in the app (layout.tsx:396), which D69 also deleted when it turned the freeze into a repair the person performs. None of it is drawn, and none of it should be restored as a lost capability.

states  streak, goal, level, day done, queued next, reduced motion, dismissed. Each state is the same panel with a different fact in it, because a person learning one shape learns all four. Queued next is also reached by using the screen: closing the first panel promotes the second. NO plan axis: none of the four triggers is gated and none differs between plans, so the absent switch is a decision.

shape  it is a panel in the shell’s notice slot, pinned directly above the composer rather than in its place, not a modal: no scrim, nothing behind it disabled, and the day’s list stays live and tappable underneath. Skipping it is a tap on the close control or simply carrying on; it never waits for an answer. When two triggers land on the same log the panels QUEUE, one at a time: the shared store gives every kind a priority (celebration-queue.ts:70-80: streak 0, achievement 1, goal completed and all done 2, level up 3), sortCelebrationQueue:82 orders by that priority and then by arrival, and enqueueCelebrationItem:184 queues an item behind whatever is active, promoting the next when the active one completes. Two panels are never on screen together, and the queued next state draws exactly that: a streak panel first, the level up behind it, in the store’s own order. The host around the panel is scenery: its rows, menus, tab bar, sidebar create and palette are wired to nothing, said here once so nobody hunts for dead controls, while the panel’s own close is a real state change and the composer is Hoje’s, prop for prop, because two documents drawing one surface draw the same surface and Hoje is the authority.

motion  under a second in total: the panel rises 12px over 280ms and the ring sweeps once over 280ms, both on the standard curve, transform only. It does not fade in, because a panel whose resting state is invisible disappears entirely the moment a timeline is paused or a frame is dropped, and the static cue is the whole point of this one. Nothing loops, nothing bounces, nothing throws particles.

static cue  the motion is never the message. The completed ring, the figure and the one line all state the fact and hold after the motion ends, so a person who looks up late, or whose screen was off, reads the same thing.

reduced motion  a dignified static state on both platforms, not a degraded one: the same panel, the same ring at its final value, the same line, arriving at full opacity with no rise and no sweep. Nothing is removed except the movement.

voice  no exclamation mark anywhere, in either locale. The line states what happened and stops; it does not congratulate, does not say keep going, and does not name the person.

accent  none. Every one of the four triggers is a completion, and completion never takes the accent, so the ring lands neutral at fg-1 and the only control is a ghost close. The ring sweeps in the accent while it is still travelling, which is the one legal frame, and resolves to fg-1 at 100 percent.

spacing  4 8 12 16 24, gap only. The panel is 24 of padding at 20 radius, the ring is 44, and the close control keeps the 44 touch token.

mock  every number carries data-mock: the streak day count, the goal figure, the level number, the day’s habit count and the allowance number inside the composer’s limit reason.
