screen  The overlay primitives in a real list rather than as specimens: the sheet at short and long content, the overflow menu in both of its presentations, and the confirmation that appears only for something irreversible. Every overlay here is opened from a row, because that is the only way to see whether it opens where it should.

states  menu, menu not overdue, sheet short, sheet long, confirm irreversible, reversible acted. A plan axis sits beside the state, free or Pro, defaulting to free, because the menu carries one real gate. Switch the width control on the menu state to see its two presentations, and switch between the two menu states to see the reschedule item appear and leave.

menu  one component, two presentations, chosen by width rather than by caller: a bottom sheet at 412, where a popover would sit under a thumb, and a popover anchored to the row at the wide width, where a sheet would move the whole screen for one small choice. The items are Hoje’s, item for item, because two documents drawing one surface draw the same surface and Hoje traced the list against both platforms: add sub habit, move parent, skip, reschedule, edit, duplicate, select, open sub habits, and delete last, destructive, behind the separator. Log and view details are absent by design: the ring already logs and the row body already opens the detail, so an item for either is a second control for something the row already does. Reschedule appears ONLY on the overdue row, because onReschedule passes only when habit.isOverdue (habit-list.tsx:906), which is why the fixture carries one overdue walk and why the menu on Leitura is one item shorter; open sub habits shows only where sub habits exist, and opens the drill, never the expand. Select stays, and stays hidden while selection is already on (this host never enters selection, so it shows); it is called out here so a third document cannot lose it by copying a seven item fixture. Dismissing changes nothing: the scrim, the catcher and Escape all leave the row exactly as it was.

plan  add a sub habit follows the plan (startAddSubHabit, habit-list.tsx:702): on free the item carries the Pro badge through Menu’s own badge slot and ROUTES to Orbit Pro, on Pro it opens creation, drawn here as the item without the badge. The badge contract is the point: a badged item stays a real button, fires onSelect, and ignores disabled while the badge is present, so the gate can never be drawn as a dead control. This is the document’s one gate, and the only reason it carries the plan axis.

first line  a live defect, drawn as fixed. An overlay used to open with its body already scrolled, so its own first line was above the fold and a person met the middle of a sentence. The sheet now opens with its scroller at the top every time it opens, and the title is outside the scroller, so the first line is structurally reachable rather than reachable by luck. The long content state is the one that proves it: it opens at the top with 14 items below.

one scroller  the second live defect, drawn as fixed. A caller used to nest its own scroll container inside the sheet's, which produced two scrollbars, a trapped inner region and a body that could not be reached at all on a short screen. The sheet owns the only scroller: content passed to it lays out and grows, and nothing inside it declares its own overflow. The long state's 14 rows are a plain column for exactly that reason.

confirmation  only for something irreversible, and the test is reversibility rather than importance. Skipping a habit advances the schedule and can be skipped back, so it acts immediately and the row simply changes. Deleting takes the subtree with it and cannot be undone from any screen, so it asks once and names what goes. A confirmation on a reversible action teaches people to dismiss confirmations, which is what makes the irreversible one dangerous.

accent  at most one filled action per overlay, and the confirmation's is destructive rather than accent, so the accent never lands on a deletion. The menu carries none: every item is an equal choice, and marking one would be a guess.

spacing  4 8 12 16 24, gap only. The sheet keeps 24 of padding and 28 of top radius, the popover 20, and every row keeps the 44 touch minimum.

contrast  status-bad carries the delete label and the destructive button, each paired with position and a word. The scrim is the token value, unchanged.

mock  the habit times, the item count inside the long sheet and the overdue word’s time carry data-mock. The overdue row also states atrasado in words in its meta strip, because colour on the ring alone is not a carrier.

tests  1 every overlay opens showing its own first line. 2 no overlay contains a second scroll container. 3 a reversible action never asks.
