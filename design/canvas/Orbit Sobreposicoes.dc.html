screen  The overlay primitives in a real list, not as specimens: the sheet at short and long content, the overflow menu in both presentations, and the confirmation for something irreversible. Every overlay opens from a row, because that is the only way to see whether it opens where it should. The plan axis exists because the menu carries one real gate.

menu  The menu is one component with two presentations, chosen by width and not by the caller: a bottom sheet at 412, where a popover would sit under a thumb, and a popover anchored to the row at wide width. The items are the items of Hoje, in order: add sub habit, move parent, skip, reschedule, edit, duplicate, select, open sub habits, and delete last, destructive, behind the separator. Log and view details are absent, because the ring already logs and the row body already opens the detail. Reschedule shows ONLY on an overdue row. Open sub habits shows only where sub habits exist. Dismissing changes nothing.

plan  Add sub habit follows the plan. On free, the item carries the Pro badge and ROUTES to Orbit Pro. On Pro, it opens creation without the badge. A badged item stays a real button and ignores disabled, so the gate is never a dead control.

sheet  The sheet owns the only scroller. Content passed to it lays out and grows, and nothing inside it declares its own overflow. The sheet opens with its scroller at the top every time, and the title sits outside the scroller, so the first line is always reachable.

confirmation  Ask only for something irreversible. The test is reversibility, not importance. Skipping a habit can be skipped back, so it acts at once and the row changes. Deleting takes the subtree with it and cannot be undone, so it asks once and names what goes.

accent  At most one filled action per overlay. The filled action of the confirmation is destructive, not accent, so the accent never lands on a deletion. The menu has no filled item: every item is an equal choice.
